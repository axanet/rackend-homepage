# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in rackend.

This repository holds the rackend homepage. It is open source under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you may read, use, fork and adapt it under the
terms of that license.

## Issues are welcome

If you find a broken link, a typo, a layout bug or something that reads wrong,
please [open an issue](https://github.com/axanet/rackend-homepage/issues/new/choose).
Questions and bug reports about rackend itself belong in the
[rackend repository](https://github.com/axanet/rackend/issues).

## Pull requests are not accepted

Changes to this repository are made only by the maintainers at axanet. Pull
requests from outside the axanet organization are closed automatically. This
is not a judgement of the change; the site is a small, curated part of the
project and we prefer to keep its design and copy in one pair of hands. If you
have a fix in mind, describe it in an issue and we will take it from there.

## For maintainers

- Develop with [mise](https://mise.jdx.dev) and bun; see the [README](README.md).
- Font files are used unmodified; their licenses live in `public/licenses/`.
